What we do

SEO, AEO, GEO and AGO in one program

Search has split into four surfaces. Your customers still type into Google, but they also ask voice assistants, read AI Overviews and ChatGPT answers, and increasingly let an AI agent compare and book for them. We optimize for all four in one program, with one strategist, one roadmap and one monthly report.

SEO

Search engine optimization

Rank on Google and Bing for the searches that bring customers. Technical health, content, links and local signals.

What we do
  • A technical audit and fixes so every page is crawlable, indexable and fast on phones
  • One page per service and location, mapped to searches like “seo for small business”
  • Content that matches search intent, with internal links that tell Google which pages matter
  • Editorial links and brand mentions from sites your market already trust

AEO

Answer engine optimization

Win the featured snippet, People Also Ask and voice answers by structuring pages so engines can quote them directly.

What we do
  • Headings written as the real questions your customers ask, pulled from Search Console and People Also Ask
  • A complete answer in the first two sentences under each heading, then the detail
  • FAQPage, Service, HowTo and Organization schema, validated on every template
  • Speakable markup and short, quotable passages for voice assistants and answer boxes

Question maps, rewritten openings, FAQ blocks and structured data across your key pages.

GEO

Generative engine optimization

Get cited inside AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini answers with entity-rich, source-worthy content.

What we do
  • Entity clarity: one consistent name, description and profile set, linked with sameAs schema
  • Quotable facts AI models can cite: price ranges, timelines, benchmarks and original data
  • Mentions on the lists, directories and publications AI assistants read about your category
  • Open access for GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended, plus an llms.txt summary
AI promptWhat is the best way to get more customers from search?
You get
An entity and citation plan, source-worthy content and a fixed prompt set tracked every month.
We measure
Share of citations across AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini against your top competitors.
Generative engine optimization
AGO

AI agent optimization

Make your business the one AI agents choose when they book, buy and compare on behalf of users: clean data, schema, APIs.

What we do
  • Clean, structured business data: services, prices, hours, service area and availability
  • LocalBusiness, Offer and Product schema plus feeds that agents can parse without guessing
  • Booking, quote and contact flows that work end to end without a human clicking through
  • Accurate listings on the platforms agents query: Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp and industry marketplaces
Agent taskFind a provider, check availability and book a call
You get
An agent-readiness audit, a structured data and listings cleanup, and a bookable path an assistant can complete.
We measure
Structured data coverage, listing accuracy and bookings or quotes that arrive through AI assistants.
