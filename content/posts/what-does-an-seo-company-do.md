An SEO company improves how often a business appears in Google, Bing and AI answers for the searches its customers make, and turns that visibility into calls, bookings and sales. In practice that means fixing the website's technical health, writing and improving the pages that answer customer searches, optimizing the Google Business Profile, earning reviews and links, and reporting results from real data. This guide explains what SEO services include, how an SEO agency differs from a consultant, and how to tell whether you need one.

## What SEO services include

Most SEO work falls into six areas. A good SEO company covers all of them, in the order that matters most for your business.

| Area | What it involves | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical SEO | Crawling, indexing, site speed, mobile layout, structured data, redirects | Google cannot rank pages it cannot crawl, render or trust |
| On-page SEO | Titles, headings, internal links, matching each page to one search intent | Tells Google exactly which search a page answers |
| Content | Service pages, location pages, guides and FAQs | Creates pages for the searches customers actually make |
| Local SEO | Google Business Profile, reviews, citations, service areas | Decides who appears in the map pack for "near me" searches |
| Off-page SEO | Earning links and mentions from relevant sites | Builds the authority needed for competitive terms |
| Reporting | Search Console, analytics, calls and form leads | Shows whether the work is producing customers |

Each area has its own page if you want the detail: [technical SEO](/technical-seo/), [on-page SEO](/on-page-seo/), [content SEO](/content-seo/), [local SEO](/local-seo/) and [link building](/link-building/).

## What an SEO company does in the first 90 days

The first three months set the direction. A typical sequence:

1. **Audit and baseline (weeks 1 to 2).** Crawl the site, check indexing in Google Search Console, review the Google Business Profile, compare competitors and record current impressions, clicks and leads.
2. **Fix what blocks rankings (weeks 2 to 6).** Indexing problems, broken pages, slow templates, missing titles, duplicate content and incorrect structured data.
3. **Build and improve pages (weeks 4 to 12).** One page per service, pages for the areas you really serve, and answers to the questions customers ask.
4. **Local and off-site work (ongoing).** Profile updates, a review request process, citations and the first earned links.
5. **Report and adjust (monthly).** Which searches gained impressions, which pages moved, and what changes next month.

## SEO agency vs SEO consultant vs SEO company

The terms overlap, and the label matters less than who does the work.

- **SEO agency or SEO company:** a team that covers strategy, technical work, content and links. Best when you want the work done for you across several areas.
- **SEO consultant:** usually one specialist who advises, audits and sets strategy, while your team or another provider implements. Best when you have in-house people who can execute. See [SEO consulting](/seo-consulting/).
- **Freelancer:** an individual who does a defined part of the work, such as content or technical fixes, often for a lower fee.

Whichever you choose, ask who will actually run your account and whether that person has experience in your industry.

## What an SEO company should not do

Some practices are red flags, whatever the price:

- **Guaranteeing rankings.** Nobody controls Google's results. Promises of "page one in 30 days" usually mean shortcuts.
- **Buying links in bulk.** Cheap link packages can lead to ranking losses that take months to recover.
- **Reporting only rankings.** Rankings without impressions, clicks and leads hide whether the work is paying off.
- **Locking you into long contracts.** A short onboarding period is reasonable; a 12-month lock-in is not.
- **Keeping your accounts.** Your Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics should stay in your name.

## Do I need an SEO company?

You probably benefit from one if most of these are true:

- Customers find businesses like yours on Google or in Google Maps.
- Competitors appear above you for your main services in your area.
- Your site gets few calls or form leads from search, or you do not know how many it gets.
- Nobody on your team has several hours a week to work on SEO consistently.

You may not need one yet if your business gets all its work from referrals, if you can spend about five hours a week on SEO yourself, or if your website has basic problems that need a rebuild first. In that case, start with our [local SEO checklist](/blog/local-seo-checklist/) and the free [SEO audit tool](/seo-tools/seo-audit-tool/).

## Is SEO worth it?

For most businesses that customers search for, yes, because organic visibility keeps producing calls without paying for each click. The return depends on three things: how many people search for your services, how much a new customer is worth, and how competitive your market is. A plumber or dentist in a mid-size city usually needs only a few extra jobs or patients a month to cover a local SEO plan. SEO is a poor fit when nobody searches for what you sell or when you need results within weeks; paid ads suit that better.

## FAQ

### What does an SEO company do?

An SEO company improves a business's visibility in search engines and AI answers. It fixes technical problems, builds and improves pages that match customer searches, optimizes the Google Business Profile, earns reviews and links, and reports results from Search Console and lead data.

### What is an SEO agency?

An SEO agency is a team of specialists that handles search engine optimization for other businesses: strategy, technical SEO, content, local SEO and link building. "SEO agency" and "SEO company" usually mean the same thing.

### What are SEO services?

SEO services are the tasks that improve how a website ranks in search: technical fixes, keyword and content work, on-page optimization, local SEO, link building and reporting. Agencies sell them as monthly plans or one-time projects such as audits.

### Do I need an SEO company or can I do SEO myself?

You can do the basics yourself with a few hours a week and good guides. An SEO company makes sense when your market is competitive, when nobody has time to work on SEO consistently, or when technical problems need a specialist.

### How long before an SEO company shows results?

Early gains on low-competition searches often appear within four to eight weeks. Competitive terms usually take four to six months. Impressions in Search Console rise first, then positions, then clicks and leads.

### What does an SEO consultant do?

An SEO consultant audits a site, sets strategy and advises a business or its team on what to change. Unlike a full-service agency, a consultant often leaves most of the implementation to the client's team.
