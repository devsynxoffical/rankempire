For twenty years, getting found meant ranking on a page a person would read. That is changing. AI assistants now take actions as well as answer questions. ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and the assistants built into phones and browsers can compare providers, check availability, fill in a quote form and book an appointment on a user's behalf. When an AI agent makes the shortlist, the user may never see a search results page or your homepage.

AI agent optimization (AGO) is the work of making your business the one those agents can understand, trust and transact with. This guide explains what AGO is, how it differs from [SEO](/seo-services/), [AEO](/aeo/) and [GEO](/geo/), what agents actually look at, and the 10-step checklist we use to make a business agent-ready.

## What AI agent optimization means

AI agent optimization is the practice of structuring a business's data, website and booking flows so that AI agents acting for a user can find it, verify it and complete a task with it: request a quote, check a price, book a slot or buy a product.

It sits one step past generative engine optimization. GEO gets your brand named in an AI answer. AGO gets your business chosen and used when the assistant moves from answering to doing.

| | SEO | AEO | GEO | AGO |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The user | Reads results and clicks | Reads one quoted answer | Reads an AI summary | Delegates the task |
| Who decides | The person | The person, guided by the snippet | The person, guided by the AI | The agent, within the person's rules |
| What wins | Relevant, authoritative pages | Quotable passages | Trusted, cited sources | Verifiable data and a completable action |
| Failure looks like | Page four | Someone else's snippet | Not mentioned | Agent skips you because it cannot confirm price, availability or how to book |

The important row is the last one. An agent that cannot confirm what you charge, when you are open, what area you serve or how to book will usually move on to a competitor whose information is clear. Agents are cautious by design, because a wrong booking is worse than no booking.

## How AI agents choose a business

Agents are still young and every platform works a little differently, but the pattern is consistent. An agent given a task such as "book a plumber for tomorrow morning" or "find three limo companies for a wedding on June 14 and get quotes" goes through roughly four stages:

1. **Discovery.** It searches the web and the platforms it trusts: Google and Bing results, Google Business Profile and Apple Maps data, review sites and industry marketplaces. Classic [local SEO](/local-seo/) still decides who is in this pool.
2. **Verification.** It checks that the business is real and fits the constraints: location and service area, hours, services offered, price range, ratings, licensing. Inconsistent data between your site and your listings is the most common reason a business drops out here.
3. **Comparison.** It ranks the shortlist against the user's criteria, usually price, availability, reviews and distance. Specific numbers beat vague claims every time.
4. **Action.** It tries to complete the task: a booking widget, a quote form, an email or a phone call through a voice agent. A form that needs a human to solve a puzzle, a booking flow hidden behind a sales call, or a phone line nobody answers ends the attempt.

Every stage favors businesses whose information is structured, consistent and machine-readable.

## What agents look for

### Structured business data

The single most useful thing you can give an agent is clean, structured data about the business. That means LocalBusiness or Organization schema on your site with the same name, address, phone, hours, service area and price range that appear on your Google Business Profile, Apple Business Connect, Bing Places and Yelp. Add Service schema for each service and Offer or Product schema where you sell defined packages with prices. Validate it with our free [schema checker](/seo-tools/schema-checker/); broken markup is worse than none because it signals an unreliable source.

### Prices, availability and terms in plain text

Agents compare. If your price is "call for a quote" and a competitor publishes "airport transfers from $95, two-hour minimum for weddings", the competitor is far easier to recommend. You do not need to publish every price, but published starting prices, minimums, deposits and cancellation terms make you comparable. Our guide on [how much SEO costs](/blog/how-much-does-seo-cost/) is an example of the format: ranges, what drives the price and what is included.

### A path to complete the task

Agents need a way to act. In order of how well they work today:

- An online booking or scheduling system with real availability.
- A short quote or contact form with clear required fields and no friction beyond basic spam protection.
- A published email address that someone answers.
- A phone line that is answered, since voice agents increasingly place calls.

The best businesses offer the first two and make sure the confirmation goes somewhere a human will see within hours.

### Consistent listings everywhere agents look

Your website is only one source. Agents cross-check Google Business Profile, Apple Maps, Bing, Yelp, industry marketplaces and review sites. Identical names, categories, hours and service areas across all of them are what verification depends on. Our [Google Business Profile checklist](/blog/google-business-profile-optimization-checklist/) covers the most important listing in detail.

### Reviews with specifics

Agents read reviews to judge fit, not just star ratings. Reviews that mention the service, the area and the outcome ("they fixed our water heater in Katy the same afternoon") help an agent match you to a request. A steady flow of specific reviews is one of the strongest signals you control.

### Open access for AI crawlers

Assistants can only use what they can read. Allow GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended and similar crawlers in robots.txt, keep key information in HTML rather than images or PDFs, and consider publishing an llms.txt file that summarizes your business, services and key pages. This site publishes [one](/llms.txt) for that reason.

## Which businesses need AGO first

Any business where the customer's task can be delegated benefits, but some are already seeing agent-driven inquiries:

- **Local services with urgent, simple jobs:** plumbers, electricians, locksmiths, towing and cleaning. "Find someone who can come today" is an ideal agent task. See our playbooks for [plumbers](/seo-for-plumbers/) and [electricians](/seo-for-electricians/).
- **Bookings with clear criteria:** restaurants, salons, gyms and clinics, where date, time and party size define the request.
- **Transportation and rentals:** [limousine companies](/seo-for-limousine-companies/), [exotic car rentals](/seo-for-exotic-car-rental/), [yacht charters](/seo-for-yacht-charter/) and [private jet charters](/seo-for-private-jet-charter/), where travelers and assistants compare price, capacity and availability for specific dates.
- **Events and venues:** [wedding venues and event companies](/seo-for-wedding-venues/), where capacity, dates and package pricing drive the shortlist.
- **E-commerce:** stores with clean product data, prices, stock and shipping terms, which shopping agents can compare and buy from.

## The 10-step agent-readiness checklist

1. **Audit your data consistency.** Compare name, address, phone, hours, categories and service area across your site, Google, Apple, Bing, Yelp and your top industry directories. Fix every mismatch.
2. **Add complete structured data.** LocalBusiness or Organization, Service for each service, Offer or Product with prices, FAQPage where the questions are unique, and sameAs links to your official profiles.
3. **Publish prices or starting prices.** Plus minimums, deposits and what is included. Agents compare; give them something to compare.
4. **State availability clearly.** Hours, lead times, booking windows and seasonal limits in plain text on the relevant pages.
5. **Make booking and quoting completable.** One short form or a real booking system, with a confirmation that reaches a person quickly.
6. **Answer the phone and the inbox.** Voice and email agents are already contacting businesses. Slow or missing responses lose the booking.
7. **Write terms in plain language.** Cancellation, refunds, insurance and requirements on a page agents can read and quote.
8. **Collect specific reviews.** Ask customers to mention the service and location. Reply to every review.
9. **Open the door to AI crawlers.** Check robots.txt, keep content in HTML and publish an llms.txt summary.
10. **Test it yourself.** Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to find, compare and book a business like yours in your area. Note who they pick, what data they cite and where your business falls out. Repeat monthly.

## FAQ

### What is AI agent optimization (AGO)?

AI agent optimization is the practice of making a business easy for AI agents to find, verify and transact with when they act on a user's behalf. It focuses on structured business data, consistent listings, published prices and availability, and booking or quote flows an agent can complete.

### How is AGO different from GEO?

Generative engine optimization gets your brand mentioned and cited in AI answers. AI agent optimization goes a step further and makes your business usable when the assistant takes an action, such as comparing quotes or booking an appointment. GEO earns the mention; AGO earns the transaction.

### Do AI agents really book appointments today?

Yes, in growing numbers. Assistants can already search, compare, fill in forms and place calls for some tasks, and booking and shopping features are expanding across the major AI platforms. Businesses with clean data and simple booking flows are the ones agents can use reliably.

### What is the quickest AGO win for a local business?

Make your business data identical across your website and every major listing, add LocalBusiness schema with your hours, service area and price range, and publish starting prices with a simple booking or quote form. Those three steps remove most of the reasons an agent would skip you.

### Does AGO replace SEO?

No. Agents discover businesses through the same search indexes and listings that SEO and local SEO improve. AGO builds on that foundation by making the business verifiable and actionable once it is found.

### How do I measure AI agent optimization?

Track structured data coverage and listing accuracy, run a monthly set of agent-style prompts across the main assistants to see whether your business is chosen, and tag bookings and inquiries that arrive through AI assistants or voice calls.
