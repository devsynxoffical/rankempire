SEO is working when the right searches show your site more often, your positions for those searches improve, and that visibility turns into calls, forms and sales, in that order. Because each step lags the one before it, many business owners judge SEO too early or by the wrong number. This guide shows how to check whether your SEO, or your SEO company, is working, what to look at in each month, and the signs that something is genuinely wrong.

## The order SEO results arrive in

SEO results show up in a predictable sequence. Knowing it is the easiest way to tell slow progress from no progress:

| Stage | What changes | Where to see it | Typical timing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Indexing | Pages are crawled and indexed | Search Console, Pages report | Days to weeks |
| 2. Impressions | Your pages appear for more searches | Search Console, Performance | Weeks 2 to 8 |
| 3. Positions | Average position improves for target queries | Search Console, Queries tab | Months 2 to 6 |
| 4. Clicks | Visits from search grow | Search Console and analytics | Once pages reach page one |
| 5. Leads | Calls, forms and bookings from organic visits | Call tracking, form and analytics events | Follows clicks |

Clicks stay low until pages reach the first page of Google, because almost nobody clicks results on page five. That is why impressions and positions are the early signals to watch.

## A real example: our own first weeks

RankEmpire's own site shows this sequence clearly. In Google Search Console, daily impressions grew from about 970 on 29 October 2026 to about 3,200 on 15 November 2026, and the daily average position improved from about 85 to about 81. Clicks over the same period stayed in single digits, because most pages still ranked beyond the first page. At that stage SEO is working in the sense that Google is showing the site for more searches and ranking it a little higher each week, but it has not yet reached the point where it produces traffic. Judging it by clicks alone would have suggested nothing was happening.

## How to check if your SEO is working in Search Console

1. **Open Performance, then Search results.** Set the date range to the last three months and compare with the previous period.
2. **Check total impressions.** A steady rise means Google is showing your site for more searches.
3. **Open the Queries tab.** Find the searches that matter to your business, such as your services and your city, and note their average position over time.
4. **Open the Pages tab.** Check that your service and location pages, not only the home page, are gaining impressions.
5. **Check the Pages report under Indexing.** Important pages should be indexed; "Discovered, currently not indexed" on key pages is a problem to fix.
6. **Match clicks to leads.** In your analytics, count calls, forms and bookings that came from organic search.

## Signs your SEO is working

- Impressions rise month over month for searches related to your services.
- Average position improves for your target keywords, even if they are not on page one yet.
- New pages get indexed within days or weeks.
- More of your pages, not just the home page, appear in search results.
- Your Google Business Profile shows more searches, calls and direction requests.
- Organic leads grow once key pages reach the first page.

## Signs your SEO is not working

Give a campaign three to four months before judging rankings, but these warning signs are worth raising at any time:

- **Impressions are flat or falling** after two to three months of work.
- **Important pages are not indexed,** or keep dropping out of the index.
- **Reports show activity, not outcomes:** lists of tasks and "links built" with no Search Console data.
- **Rankings are reported only for keywords nobody searches** or for your brand name.
- **Your agency cannot explain** what they changed and why a metric moved.
- **Traffic grows but leads do not,** which usually means the wrong searches or pages that do not convert.

## How to tell if your SEO company is working

Ask your SEO company for these every month. A good one will provide them without being asked:

- Search Console impressions, clicks and average position, compared with the previous period
- Positions for an agreed list of target keywords
- Pages created or improved, and what changed on each
- Indexing status of new and important pages
- Organic leads: calls, forms and bookings
- The plan for next month and why

If a report contains none of these, ask for access to your own Search Console and analytics. You should always own those accounts.

## What to do if your SEO is not working

1. **Check the foundations.** Make sure key pages are indexed, load quickly on mobile and each target one clear search.
2. **Check intent.** If the wrong searches bring visitors, retarget pages to the searches your customers make.
3. **Check authority.** If pages are well built but stuck beyond page three, the site likely needs real links and mentions from relevant sites; buying cheap link packages usually makes this worse.
4. **Check your Google Business Profile** if you depend on local searches.
5. **Get a second opinion.** An independent [SEO audit](/seo-audit/) shows what is holding the site back.

## How long before SEO should work?

Most sites see impressions rise within the first two months, meaningful position gains for target searches within four to six months, and lead growth after that. Low-competition and local searches move faster; competitive national terms take longer. See [how much SEO costs](/blog/how-much-does-seo-cost/) for what to expect at each budget.

## FAQ

### How do I know if my SEO is working?

Check Google Search Console. If impressions are rising, average positions for your target searches are improving and important pages are indexed, SEO is working. Clicks and leads follow once pages reach the first page of results.

### How do I tell if my SEO company is working?

Ask for monthly Search Console data, positions for agreed target keywords, a list of pages changed and organic leads. If your company reports only tasks completed or links built, without this data, it is hard to know whether the work is paying off.

### Does SEO really work?

Yes, for businesses whose customers search for what they sell, but it takes months and depends on the site's technical health, content and authority. It does not work when nobody searches for the service or when shortcuts such as bought links are used.

### Why is my SEO not working?

The most common reasons are pages that are not indexed, content that does not match what people search for, one page targeting too many topics, low authority from few real links, and judging results too early.

### How long does it take to see SEO results?

Impressions usually rise within weeks, positions improve over two to six months, and clicks and leads grow once pages reach the first page. Competitive searches take longer.

### How do I track SEO results?

Use Google Search Console for impressions, clicks, positions and indexing, analytics for organic visits and leads, and call tracking or form events for conversions. Compare month over month rather than day to day.
