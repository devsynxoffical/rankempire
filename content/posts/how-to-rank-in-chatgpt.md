ChatGPT does not have a ranking you can buy or a setting you can switch on. When it searches the web, it picks a handful of sources it can reach, trust and quote. Ranking in ChatGPT means becoming one of those sources for the questions your buyers ask.

## How do you rank in ChatGPT?

To rank in ChatGPT, make sure OpenAI's search crawler can reach your site, rank well in the search results ChatGPT draws on, answer each buyer question directly on its own page, and get your brand named on other trusted sites. ChatGPT cites pages that are easy to find, easy to quote and confirmed by other sources.

## How ChatGPT chooses which sites to mention

ChatGPT answers in two ways. For many questions it answers from what the model learned in training. When a question needs current or specific information, it searches the web and cites the pages it used. OpenAI says ChatGPT search uses third-party search providers, including Bing, together with its own crawler, OAI-SearchBot.

That gives you three levers:

| Lever | What it affects | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search access | Whether your page can be found and cited when ChatGPT searches | Allow OAI-SearchBot, get indexed in Bing, keep pages fast and crawlable |
| Brand presence | Whether the model and its sources already associate your brand with the topic | Get named in reviews, directories, list articles and discussions |
| Answer quality | Whether your page is the easiest one to quote | A direct answer under a question heading, tables, steps and clear facts |

## The 8 steps

1. **Allow the right crawlers.** In robots.txt, allow OAI-SearchBot (ChatGPT search) and, if you are happy for your content to be used in training, GPTBot. Blocking OAI-SearchBot keeps your pages out of ChatGPT's search answers.
2. **Get indexed in Bing.** Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools, submit your sitemap and use IndexNow so new pages are picked up quickly.
3. **Answer one question per page section.** Use the buyer's question as an H2 and answer it in the first 40 to 60 words, then add detail. Our [answer engine optimization](/aeo/) service is built around this structure.
4. **Use tables and numbered steps.** Prices, comparisons and timelines in a table, and how-to answers as numbered steps, are the easiest parts of a page to quote.
5. **State facts plainly.** Who you serve, what it costs, how long it takes and one published result, all in plain text on the page. AI tools repeat facts that are written clearly and consistently.
6. **Make your brand an entity.** Use the same name, description, logo and contact details on your site, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn and directories, and connect them with Organization schema and sameAs links.
7. **Earn mentions on other sites.** Reviews, directory profiles, "best of" list articles, podcasts and helpful answers in communities are where AI tools confirm that a brand is real and recommended.
8. **Track prompts every month.** Write down the 5 to 10 questions your buyers would ask ChatGPT, run them monthly and record whether you are named and which page is cited.

## What does not work

- **Stuffing pages with "ChatGPT" keywords.** ChatGPT answers the buyer's question, not the word ChatGPT.
- **Hidden text or prompt instructions on the page.** They are ignored or treated as manipulation.
- **Buying mentions on low-quality sites.** Spam links and paid placements on link farms do not build the trust AI tools look for.
- **Expecting overnight results.** Search access changes can show within weeks; brand presence builds over months.

## How long does it take?

Pages that are already indexed and well structured can start being cited within a few weeks of a ChatGPT search crawl. Being named by ChatGPT for broad questions such as "best agency for X" usually takes longer, because it depends on how often other trusted sites mention your brand.

## FAQ

### Can I pay to rank in ChatGPT?

No. ChatGPT does not sell placement in its answers. You earn mentions by being easy to find, easy to quote and trusted by other sources.

### Does ChatGPT use Google or Bing?

OpenAI says ChatGPT search uses third-party search providers, including Bing, and its own crawler, OAI-SearchBot. Being indexed in Bing and allowing OAI-SearchBot both help.

### Should I block GPTBot?

Blocking GPTBot stops your content being used to train OpenAI's models, but it does not stop ChatGPT search. To appear in ChatGPT search answers, keep OAI-SearchBot allowed.

### How do I check if ChatGPT mentions my business?

Write down the questions your buyers ask, run them in ChatGPT with search enabled each month and record whether your brand is named and which page is cited.

### Is ranking in ChatGPT different from SEO?

It uses the same foundations: crawlable pages, direct answers and a trusted brand. The difference is that AI answers reward quotable facts and mentions across the web even more than classic rankings do.
