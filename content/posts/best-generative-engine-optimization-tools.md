Generative engine optimization tools show whether AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews mention your brand, and which pages they cite. There is no official ranking report for AI answers, so these tools estimate visibility by running prompts and recording the results. This guide covers what to look for and the main options.

## What are the best generative engine optimization tools?

The best generative engine optimization tools combine free data you already own, such as Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools, with a prompt tracker that checks AI answers for your brand every week. Choose a tracker by the AI engines it covers, how it picks prompts, how often it checks and whether it shows the cited pages.

## How to choose a GEO tool

| Criterion | Why it matters | What to ask |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engines covered | Buyers use different assistants | Does it check ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode? |
| Prompt control | Results depend on the exact question | Can you add your own buyer prompts, not only generated ones? |
| Citations | You need to know which page earned the mention | Does it show the URLs cited, not just brand mentions? |
| Frequency | AI answers change often | Daily, weekly or monthly checks? |
| Competitors | Visibility is relative | Can you compare share of mentions against named competitors? |
| Location and language | Answers vary by market | Can you set the country and language? |

## The main tools

1. **Google Search Console (free).** Google counts clicks and impressions from AI Overviews and AI Mode in the Performance report for web search. It does not separate them, but it is the only first-party data on how your pages perform in Google's AI features.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools (free).** Shows how your pages perform in Bing, whose index feeds Microsoft Copilot and is one of the search sources ChatGPT uses. Use it with IndexNow to get new pages crawled quickly.
3. **Semrush (AI visibility features).** Adds AI search visibility, mentions and cited pages next to its keyword and backlink data, which suits teams that already use it for SEO.
4. **Ahrefs Brand Radar.** Tracks how often brands are mentioned in AI answers and compares share of voice against competitors, alongside Ahrefs' backlink and keyword data.
5. **Profound.** A dedicated AI visibility platform aimed at larger brands, focused on how assistants describe and cite a brand across many prompts.
6. **Peec AI.** Tracks brand mentions, citations and sentiment across AI assistants for a list of prompts you control.
7. **Otterly.AI.** Monitors a set of prompts across AI search engines and reports brand mentions and cited links over time.
8. **A manual prompt log (free).** A spreadsheet of 5 to 10 buyer questions, run in each assistant once a month, with the answer, whether you were named and the cited URL. It is slow, but it is exact and costs nothing.

Features and coverage change quickly in this category, so check each tool's current engines, limits and pricing before you buy. RankEmpire has no affiliation with any of these tools.

## What the tools cannot do

- **They do not show real prompt volumes.** No tool knows how many people ask ChatGPT a given question, so treat prompt lists as samples, not traffic forecasts.
- **They cannot make you visible.** A tracker tells you where you stand. Being cited still depends on crawlable pages, clear answers and mentions on trusted sites, which our [generative engine optimization](/geo/) service covers.
- **Results vary by user.** Assistants personalize and change answers, so look at trends over several weeks.

## FAQ

### What is a GEO tool?

A GEO tool checks whether AI assistants mention your brand and cite your pages for a list of questions, and tracks how that changes over time.

### Is there a free generative engine optimization tool?

Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools are free and show search performance that feeds AI features. A monthly manual prompt log is also free. Dedicated trackers are paid.

### Can a GEO tool show how many people ask ChatGPT a question?

No. AI companies do not publish prompt volumes, so tools track sample prompts, not search demand.

### Do I need a GEO tool if I already do SEO?

If AI answers matter for your buyers, yes. SEO tools show rankings in search results, while GEO tools show mentions and citations inside AI answers.

### How often should I check AI visibility?

Weekly with a tracker, or monthly with a manual log. AI answers change often, so judge progress by the trend over several weeks.
