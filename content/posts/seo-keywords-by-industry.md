The best SEO keywords for a local business are the exact phrases customers type when they are ready to hire: the service, the problem, the price question and the place. This guide gives starter keyword lists for 15 local industries, grouped by search intent, and shows how to turn each group into the right page. Use them as seeds, then confirm which ones already show your site in Google Search Console.

## How to use these keyword lists

Every list below is split into five groups, because each group belongs on a different kind of page:

| Group | What it signals | Where it belongs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Service keywords | Ready to hire for a specific job | One service page per job |
| Urgent keywords | Needs help now | Emergency page and Google Business Profile |
| Cost keywords | Comparing options | Pricing section or a cost guide |
| Local keywords | Wants someone nearby | Location and service area pages |
| Question keywords | Researching | FAQs and blog guides |

Add your city or neighborhood to service and urgent keywords ("water heater repair Katy"). Google treats "near me" searches by location automatically, so you rarely need to write "near me" on the page; you need accurate service areas and a strong [Google Business Profile](/google-business-profile-seo/).

## Turn keywords into pages

1. **Pick one primary keyword per page.** Put it at the start of the title, in the H1 and in the first sentence.
2. **Use close variants as secondary keywords.** "Water heater repair", "water heater replacement" and "fix water heater" can share one page.
3. **Answer the questions on the page.** Add a short FAQ with the question keywords and direct answers; it helps featured snippets and AI answers, as explained in [AEO vs GEO vs SEO](/blog/aeo-vs-geo-vs-seo/).
4. **Check real demand.** Search Console shows which searches already show your site. Generate more ideas with our free [keyword research tool](/seo-tools/keyword-research-tool/) and check where you rank with the [keyword rank checker](/seo-tools/keyword-rank-checker/).
5. **Build local pages carefully.** Only create a city page where you have real work and reviews there. Our [SEO for contractors](/blog/seo-for-contractors/) guide covers how.

## FAQ

### What are the best SEO keywords for a local business?

The best keywords combine a specific service with a location or an urgent need, such as "emergency plumber Houston" or "ceramic coating Scottsdale". They have lower search volume than broad terms but far higher intent, and they are easier to rank for.

### Should I put "near me" in my keywords?

Usually not on the page itself. Google interprets "near me" using the searcher's location and your Google Business Profile, service areas and reviews. Focus on accurate location details and pages for the areas you serve.

### How many keywords should one page target?

One primary keyword and a handful of close variants with the same intent. If two keywords need different answers, they need different pages.

### How do I find which keywords my site already ranks for?

Open Google Search Console, go to Performance and look at the Queries tab. It lists every search that showed your site, with impressions and average position. Pages at positions 11 to 30 are the quickest wins.

### Do I need a paid keyword tool?

Not to start. Search Console, Google's autocomplete, People Also Ask boxes and your own customer calls give plenty of keywords. Paid tools add search volume and difficulty data, which helps when choosing between many options.
