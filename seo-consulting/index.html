In depth

What is SEO consulting?

SEO consulting is an expert advisory service where an experienced SEO specialist assesses your search performance.

Recommends strategy and guides your team or vendors in carrying it out, rather than doing all the hands on work themselves. Consulting suits businesses that have internal marketers, developers or writers but need senior direction, an independent second opinion, help with a specific challenge such as a migration or traffic drop, or training to build SEO skills in house. It focuses on decisions, priorities and knowledge transfer.

A consulting engagement can be a single strategy session, a defined project or an ongoing advisory retainer with regular calls. Typical work includes reviewing roadmaps, diagnosing ranking or traffic declines, advising on site migrations, evaluating agency proposals, training content and development teams and reviewing work before it goes live. RankEmpire consultants work across local, e-commerce and SaaS sites, so advice draws on how different business models approach search. Recommendations come with reasoning, so your team understands why, not just what.

Why it matters

Why SEO consulting matters for your team

01

Senior expertise without a full time hire

Hiring an experienced SEO lead is expensive and slow. Consulting gives your team access to senior judgment for strategy and difficult problems, while day to day execution stays with the people who already know your business. You pay for guidance when you need it.

02

An independent perspective

Internal teams and existing agencies can develop blind spots. An outside consultant reviews your situation without attachment to past decisions and gives an honest assessment of what is working and what is not. That clarity helps you decide where to invest next.

03

Build lasting skills in house

Consulting includes explanation and training, so your marketers, writers and developers learn to make good SEO decisions themselves. That knowledge stays with your company after the engagement ends. Your team grows more confident and depends less on outside help for routine decisions.

SEO consulting is for teams that can do the work but want expert direction: a marketing manager, a developer, an in-house content writer, or a founder who wants a second opinion before spending on an agency. A good SEO consultant sets the strategy, reviews what your team ships and catches problems before they cost rankings.

What our expert SEO consulting services include

A full SEO audit and a prioritized 90-day plan, monthly strategy sessions, review of new pages and content before they go live, technical guidance for your developers, keyword and content planning, and migration or redesign planning when you change platforms. You get a named senior consultant, not a rotating account team.

Independent SEO consultant vs SEO agency

An SEO consultant advises and your team executes; an agency does the work for you. Consulting costs less each month and builds skills in-house, but it only works if someone internally has time to implement. If you lack that capacity, a full SEO services plan is usually the better value. Our guide on how to choose an SEO company covers the trade-off.

When to hire an SEO consultant

The most common moments are before a website redesign or migration, after a traffic drop, when building an in-house team, when a previous agency produced reports but no growth, and when a business wants an independent review of an agency proposal. Each benefits from experienced judgment more than extra hours.

SEO consultancy for AI search

Search now includes featured snippets, AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity answers, and AI agents that book on a user's behalf. Our consultancy covers answer engine optimization, generative engine optimization and AI agent optimization so your team prepares for all of them in one plan.

How SEO consulting is priced

Consulting is usually priced as a monthly retainer for a set number of sessions and reviews, or as a fixed project for an audit or migration. We quote after a free initial review so the scope matches what your team can implement. See SEO packages for our full-service plans by comparison. The first SEO consultation is free: a 30-minute call and a written review of your site's biggest opportunities.

SEO consultant vs SEO specialist vs SEO expert

The titles overlap. An SEO specialist usually executes one area, such as technical fixes, content or links, often in-house. An SEO consultant sets the strategy across all of them, audits the site and reviews your team's work. "SEO expert" is a label anyone can use, so judge it by evidence: real Search Console results, a clear method and references. RankEmpire works as the consultant, while your team or ours does the specialist work.

How to vet an SEO consultant

Ask for Search Console screenshots from real clients, shared with permission, not rank tracker charts. Ask what they would do in the first 90 days and in what order, how they earn links, and who owns the work if you stop. Walk away from guaranteed rankings, results promised in 30 days or secret methods. Our guide on how to choose an SEO company lists the twelve questions we suggest, and our own verified client results are all real Search Console reports.
