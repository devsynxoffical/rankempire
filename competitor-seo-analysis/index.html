In depth

What is competitor SEO analysis?

Competitor SEO analysis is the process of studying the websites that outrank you in search results to understand why they perform well and where you.

Can compete more effectively. It examines their keyword coverage, top pages, content depth, site structure, technical setup, backlink profiles and, for local businesses, their Google Business Profiles and reviews. The aim is not to copy competitors but to identify gaps, benchmark the effort needed to compete and find opportunities they have missed or handled poorly.

Your search competitors are not always your business competitors. A local roofing company may compete with directories and home improvement publishers for informational searches, while a SaaS startup may face review sites and large incumbents. Competitor SEO analysis starts by identifying who actually ranks for your priority terms, then compares their strengths and weaknesses with yours. The findings show which keywords to target, what content to create or improve, where links are needed and how long competing might realistically take.

Why it matters

Why competitor SEO analysis matters for your strategy

Understand what you are up against

Seeing the links, content and structure behind competing pages shows the realistic effort required to rank. That helps you set budgets and expectations based on evidence from your market rather than guesses. It also shows which battles are worth fighting now.

Find gaps worth filling

Competitors leave openings, such as topics covered thinly, outdated pages or questions nobody answers. Analysis surfaces those gaps so you can target searches where you have a better chance to win. These are often faster wins than attacking a competitor's strongest pages head on.

Learn from what works

Patterns among top ranking pages, such as formats, page types and link sources, reveal what search engines reward in your market. You can apply those lessons while adding your own expertise and perspective. Copying is not the goal, understanding the pattern is.

Guide

Competitor SEO analysis: how to find why rivals outrank you

Identify your real search competitorsCompare keywords and pagesCompare authorityCompare local signalsTurn findings into a plan

Competitor SEO analysis compares your site with the pages that rank above you to find exactly what they do better: keywords, content, links, technical health and local signals. It turns "they rank higher" into a list of specific fixes.

Identify your real search competitors

Your search competitors are the sites ranking for your target keywords, which are often different from your business competitors. Directories, publishers and national agencies frequently hold the top spots alongside local rivals.

Compare keywords and pages

Find the keywords competitors rank for that you do not, and compare their pages with yours: depth, structure, questions answered, prices and proof. Use our keyword research tool and keyword rank checker for quick checks.

Compare authority

Count referring domains and look at where competitors earn links and mentions: directories, press, partners, guest articles. These are often the most reusable opportunities. Check profiles with our backlink checker.

Compare local signals

For local searches, compare Google Business Profile categories, review counts and ratings, photos and posting frequency, and how many service area pages each competitor has.

Turn findings into a plan

Rank gaps by impact and effort: quick wins on pages that already rank on page two, content gaps with clear demand, and longer authority work. Our SEO consulting and SEO audit services include this analysis.
