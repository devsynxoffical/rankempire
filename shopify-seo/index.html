In depth

What does a Shopify SEO expert do?

A Shopify SEO expert makes a Shopify store rank for the searches its buyers use, then turns that traffic into orders.

In practice that means collection pages that target category searches, product pages with unique descriptions and full product data, a clean URL and canonical setup for Shopify's duplicate /collections/.../products/ paths, a fast theme with fewer apps, product schema for price, stock and review stars, and content that answers buying questions. Unlike generic SEO, the work follows Shopify's own limits: fixed URL prefixes, theme code and app bloat.

Most stores do not need everything at once. The fastest gains come from fixing what blocks indexing and collections first, then improving the products that already sell. We measure progress in Google Search Console and in Shopify revenue from organic search, and agree a plan that fits your catalog and team, month to month.

Guide

Shopify SEO: what to fix first

Make collection pages worth rankingFix Shopify's duplicate product URLsWrite product pages for buyers, not suppliersSpeed: fewer apps, lighter themeMeasure organic revenue

Shopify gives stores a solid base, but a few platform habits hold most stores back. This is the order we fix them in.

Make collection pages worth ranking

Collections usually match the searches with the most buyers, such as "men's trail running shoes". Add a short, useful introduction, clear filters and FAQs, and link related collections. A collection with no text rarely beats a marketplace.

Fix Shopify's duplicate product URLs

Shopify links to products through /collections/name/products/ paths as well as /products/. Make sure internal links point to the canonical /products/ URL and that tag and filter pages do not flood the index. Our technical SEO basics guide explains canonicals and indexing.

Write product pages for buyers, not suppliers

Replace manufacturer copy on the products that sell with unique descriptions, real photos, sizing and shipping answers. Product schema with price, stock and reviews helps earn rich results.

Speed: fewer apps, lighter theme

Every app can add scripts to every page. Remove the ones you do not use, compress images and test on mobile. Faster stores rank and convert better.

Measure organic revenue

Track orders from organic search in Shopify and GA4, not just rankings. See how to tell if your SEO is working, our ecommerce SEO services and how much SEO costs.
