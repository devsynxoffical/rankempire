In depth

What does an SEO company do?

An SEO company improves how often and how prominently a business appears in organic search results on Google and other search engines.

The work combines technical fixes, content, on-page optimization, local listings and link acquisition, all guided by keyword research and data from tools like Google Search Console and Google Analytics. A good SEO company starts by finding what is holding a site back, then builds a prioritized plan that matches the business model, whether that means ranking a single location for service searches or growing category traffic for an online store.

Day to day, an SEO company audits crawlability and indexing, maps keywords to pages, rewrites titles and content, improves internal linking, earns mentions from relevant sites and reports on what changed. At RankEmpire we work with local businesses, e-commerce brands and SaaS companies, so the mix of tasks shifts with the goal. A plumber needs map pack visibility and service area pages. A store needs clean category architecture and product page fixes. A SaaS company needs content that captures problem and comparison searches from buyers who are evaluating tools.

Why it matters

Why hiring an SEO company matters for your business

Search is where buyers start

Many purchases and service calls begin with a search. When your business does not show up for the terms buyers use, those leads go to competitors who do. An SEO company makes sure the right pages exist and are clearly understood by search engines for those searches.

Compounding results

Paid ads stop the moment the budget does. Organic visibility built on sound pages, useful content and earned links keeps working month after month, which lowers your reliance on paid channels over time and gives each new page a stronger base to rank from.

Specialist skills in one team

SEO spans development, writing, analytics and outreach. Hiring all of those roles in house is expensive and slow. An SEO company gives you access to each skill set and the tooling behind it, coordinated around one plan with clear priorities and one point of contact.

An SEO company improves how often your business appears in Google, Google Maps and AI answers for the searches your customers make, then turns that visibility into calls, leads and sales. This guide explains what an SEO company actually does, how it differs from an SEO agency or consultant, how to find a good one near you or remotely, and what it should cost.

What does an SEO company do?

A good SEO company fixes the technical health of your site, maps every important search to one page, improves or writes those pages, optimizes your Google Business Profile, earns links and mentions from other sites, and reports results from Google Search Console. Work runs in a fixed order: audit, foundations, then content and authority. Our guide to what an SEO company does covers each service, the first 90 days and the red flags to avoid.

SEO company vs SEO agency vs SEO consultant

"SEO company" and "SEO agency" usually mean the same thing: a team that does the work for you. An SEO consultant sets the strategy and reviews your team's work while your staff implements it. Choose a company or agency when you need execution, and a consultant when you have in-house people who can ship changes.

Finding an SEO company near you

Searching "SEO company near me" shows agencies close to you, but location matters less than whether the team understands your market. A remote company can rank you anywhere if it checks your visibility across your real service area with a rank grid. RankEmpire works remotely with US businesses and runs local programs in cities including New York, Chicago, Houston, Phoenix, Dallas and Los Angeles; see all locations.

How much does an SEO company cost?

Most small businesses pay $500 to $2,500 a month, multi-location and e-commerce programs $2,000 to $8,000, and national programs more. RankEmpire's local plans start at $599 a month, month to month after a three-month onboarding. Our guide on how much SEO costs explains what drives the price, and our SEO packages list what each plan includes.

How to choose the best SEO company

Ask for real Search Console results shared with permission, a named strategist who does the work, a 90-day plan in order, how they earn links and who owns the work if you stop. Avoid guaranteed rankings, results promised in 30 days and long lock-in contracts. Our guide on how to choose an SEO company lists twelve questions, and our verified results show the reports we share. For how to judge "best SEO company" rankings, read who is the best SEO company in the USA.

How long does an SEO company take to show results?

Expect early movement on long-tail and local searches in 60 to 90 days, meaningful gains on core terms in four to six months, and compounding growth after that. Impressions in Search Console rise first, then positions, then clicks and leads, as shown in our indexing case study.
