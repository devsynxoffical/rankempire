Why lawyers need SEO

People searching for a lawyer are usually facing a stressful problem with a deadline attached. An arrest, a custody dispute, a contract gone wrong, or an estate that needs planning sends them to Google with phrases that describe their situation more than a legal practice area. Urgent matters like criminal charges lead to fast calls to firms that answer the phone, while estate planning or business formation clients research over weeks. Most compare a few firms, read reviews about communication and outcomes, and look closely at attorney biographies before scheduling a consultation.

Strategy

Our SEO strategy for lawyers

A clear plan, owned by one senior strategist, reviewed with you every month.

4steps
90day plan
1owner
Start with a free audit
  1. 1
    Step 01Week 1

    Set up the profile for consultations

    Choose precise categories such as family law attorney or criminal justice attorney rather than a generic lawyer category, list practice areas as services, and keep hours accurate. Add office and attorney photos and a consultation booking link. Answer common questions about free consultations and payment options in the Q and A.

  2. 2
    Step 02Weeks 2 to 4

    Build practice area clusters

    Create a main page for each practice area and supporting pages for specific matters and process questions. Link them together logically. Write in plain language, cite relevant state law accurately, and include clear calls to schedule a consultation without promising outcomes.

  3. 3
    Step 03Month 2 onward

    Earn reviews within bar rules

    Ask satisfied clients for reviews after a matter closes, following your state's rules on solicitation and testimonials. Never offer anything in exchange. Reply without revealing confidential details or confirming the specifics of anyone's case, since attorney confidentiality applies to online responses too.

  4. 4
    Step 04Every month

    Build authority through legal and civic ties

    Maintain profiles in reputable legal directories, participate in bar association committees, and speak at community or business events. Writing for local publications and teaching continuing education sessions earns links and mentions that signal real expertise to search engines and prospective clients.

State bar advertising rules govern testimonials, specialist claims, and disclaimers, and confidentiality duties apply to review replies, so all content should be reviewed against the rules where the firm practices.
