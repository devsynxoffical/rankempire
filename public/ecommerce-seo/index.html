Overview

Online stores lose organic revenue in quiet ways: duplicate product variants, thin category pages, filters that create thousands of useless URLs and missing product schema. We fix the store first, then build the category and content strategy that captures buyers at every stage.

In depth

What is e-commerce SEO?

E-commerce SEO is the process of optimizing an online store so its category pages.

Product pages and supporting content rank in organic search for the terms shoppers use. It covers site architecture, faceted navigation, crawl budget, product schema, unique product descriptions, internal linking and content that answers questions before a purchase. Unlike a small brochure site, a store can have thousands of URLs created by filters, variants and pagination, so much of the work is making sure search engines index the pages that matter and ignore the ones that do not.

Much e-commerce search traffic lands on category pages, because shoppers often search broad terms like waterproof hiking boots before they pick a specific product. Strong e-commerce SEO gives those categories helpful intro copy, clear filters and links to related collections. Product pages need original descriptions, structured data for price and availability, and good images. We work with stores on Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce and custom platforms, adapting the approach to each platform's limits, such as Shopify's fixed URL structure or WooCommerce plugin conflicts.

Why it matters

Why e-commerce SEO matters for your store

01

Lower dependence on paid shopping

Shopping ads and marketplace fees tend to rise as competition grows. Organic traffic to category and product pages gives a store a steady source of visits that does not charge per click, which helps protect margins over time. It also gives you leverage when ad costs spike during busy seasons.

02

Capture shoppers earlier

Buyers research before they purchase. Buying guides, comparison pages and helpful category content reach shoppers while they are still deciding, and bring them to your store instead of a competitor or a large marketplace. Those early visits also build brand recognition that pays off when shoppers return ready to buy.

03

Fix problems that hide products

Duplicate content, filter generated URLs and poor out of stock handling can quietly block products from ranking. E-commerce SEO finds and fixes these structural problems so search engines can find and trust your best pages. Fixing them often helps every product in the affected categories at once.

What you get each month

What you get every month, visible in your dashboard.

Week 1

Category page optimization

We map keywords to collections, write helpful intro and FAQ copy, refine titles and headings, and strengthen links between related categories so your highest value pages have a better chance to rank.

Week 2

Faceted navigation controls

We review how filters, sorting and variants generate URLs, then set canonical tags, noindex rules or crawl directives so search engines focus on useful pages instead of endless duplicates.

Week 3

Product schema and feed checks

We implement or repair Product structured data for price, availability and reviews, and check consistency with your Merchant Center feed to support rich results and free listings.

Week 4

Buying guide content

We plan and write guides and comparison articles around pre-purchase questions in your niche, linking them to the relevant categories and products to support both traffic and conversions.
