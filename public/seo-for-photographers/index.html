Why photographers need SEO

Photography clients search with a specific event or purpose in mind. Couples look for a wedding photographer months in advance, parents want newborn or family portraits, professionals need headshots for a new job, and businesses look for product or real estate photography. Most clients spend time browsing portfolios, comparing styles, and checking pricing before reaching out. Availability on a specific date can make some searches urgent. Reviews describing how the photographer made people feel comfortable often matter as much as the images themselves.

Strategy

Our SEO strategy for photographers

A clear plan, owned by one senior strategist, reviewed with you every month.

4steps
90day plan
1owner
Start with a free audit
  1. 1
    Step 01Week 1

    Show style and range on the profile

    Upload a curated selection of recent images, define service areas, and list each type of photography offered. Use posts to share recent sessions and booking availability. A strong visual profile helps clients quickly judge style and decide whether to explore further.

  2. 2
    Step 02Weeks 2 to 4

    Optimize galleries and niche pages

    Create pages for each photography type and add descriptive text, image file names, and alt text to galleries. Write short stories about sessions, including locations and goals. This context helps search engines understand the images and match them with relevant searches.

  3. 3
    Step 03Month 2 onward

    Collect reviews about the experience

    Request reviews a few days after delivering the final gallery, when excitement is highest, and invite clients to mention comfort in front of the camera, communication, and favorite moments. These details tell nervous future clients what a session actually feels like, which strongly influences who they book.

  4. 4
    Step 04Every month

    Build relationships with vendors and venues

    Connect with wedding venues, planners, florists, and real estate agents, and share credited images they can use on their own sites. Get listed on venue preferred vendor pages and take part in styled shoots or local events. These partnerships bring referrals and relevant links that strengthen local visibility.

Photographers should obtain model releases before using client images in marketing and be careful with copyright, licensing terms, and permissions when sharing work publicly.
